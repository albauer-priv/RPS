# Taper and Peaking Evidence

Evidence backing for the taper week content rules and backward-planning logic in
`macrocycle_and_peak_window_rules.md` and `SKILL.md`.

Cross-references to the shared durability evidence library are noted where entries already exist.

---

## Core scientific sources

| id | authors | year | title | outlet | locator | key finding for taper rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tap_core_001` | Bosquet L, Montpetit J, Arvisais D, Mujika I | 2007 | Effects of tapering on performance: a meta-analysis | Med Sci Sports Exerc 39(8):1358–65 | https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17762369/ — DOI 10.1249/mss.0b013e31806010e0 | Meta-analysis of 27 studies: optimal taper = 2 weeks, volume reduced 41–60% exponentially, **training intensity and frequency maintained**; overall performance gain 0.59 effect size |
| `tap_core_002` | Mujika I, Padilla S | 2003 | Scientific bases for precompetition tapering strategies | Med Sci Sports Exerc 35(7):1182–7 | DOI 10.1249/01.mss.0000074448.73931.11 — also in durability library as `dur_core_012` | Volume reduction up to 60–90%, **intensity maintained**, frequency reduced no more than 20%; performance improvement ~3% (range 0.5–6%); defines taper as "progressive nonlinear reduction of training load to reduce physiological and psychological stress" |

**Intensity maintenance is the single most critical taper rule** (both tap_core_001 and tap_core_002 agree):
reducing intensity causes 20–30% performance loss; all other variables are secondary.

## Applied practitioner sources

| id | authors | year | title | outlet | locator | key finding for taper rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tap_applied_001` | Friel J | 2018 | The Cyclist's Training Bible (5th ed.) | VeloPress | also in durability library as `dur_core_017` | Backward planning from A race: anchor the race date, work backward — taper → peak → build → base; B events = rehearsal/load markers, not independent peaks; practical 7–14 day taper for most events |
| `tap_applied_002` | Gallagher S | 2016/2024 | How Cyclists Should Plan for Multiple Peaks in a Season | TrainingPeaks blog | https://www.trainingpeaks.com/blog/how-cyclists-should-plan-for-multiple-peaks-in-a-season/ | Peak phase 3–4 weeks; taper 7–10 days ("icing on the cake"); fitness does not change in taper, freshness does; B events placed inside 8-week build window as fitness markers; ≥ 12 weeks between A events for separate macrocycles |
| `tap_applied_003` | TORQ Fitness | 2022 | Periodisation & Peaking 3: Peaking For Competition | torqfitness.co.uk | https://www.torqfitness.co.uk/news/periodization-and-peaking-3-peaking-your-form | Taper = "progressive reduction in training load … to reduce physiological and psychological stress resulting in a peak in performance"; practical breakdown: volume first variable to reduce, intensity maintained or slightly elevated, frequency reduced last; 50–75% volume reduction for 6-day taper; supercompensation window framework |

## Transfer boundaries

- tap_core_001 and tap_core_002 are derived from competitive athletes across endurance sports; direct ultra/brevet data is limited — but both conclude that intensity maintenance is **sport-agnostic** within endurance
- Friel (tap_applied_001) is practitioner guidance for road cycling / triathlon; valid for ultra/brevet by extension when scaled for longer events (longer taper window)
- For ultra/brevet events (> 12 hours): the minimum taper window extends to 2–3 weeks (vs 7–14 days for shorter events) — this is supported by tap_core_002's finding that optimal taper duration varies by event duration, not by a 2-week universal rule
- None of these sources are authoritative over RPS deterministic load governance; they justify structural decisions (phase designation, taper duration), not numeric prescriptions

---

## What this evidence does and does not justify

**Justifies:**
- Volume 41–60% reduction in taper weeks (`tap_core_001`)
- Intensity maintenance requirement — the "halten" principle (`tap_core_001`, `tap_core_002`)
- Frequency reduction as last lever, ≥ 80% maintained (`tap_core_002`)
- B event as final specificity stimulus before taper (`tap_applied_001`, `tap_applied_002`)
- Near-peak form maintainable 2–4 weeks when taper is structured correctly (`tap_applied_002`)
- Minimum 2–3 week taper for ultra/brevet events (`tap_core_002` extended by duration scaling)

**Does not justify:**
- Specific kJ targets, power percentages, or session prescriptions in taper weeks
- Overriding deterministic load bands or availability constraints
- Taper rules for non-endurance events
